We will also invest in continuous model validation and retraining strategies utilizing our extensive dataset of 2.5 million anonymized SME transactions. Our Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) stands at £180, with a Lifetime Value (LTV) of £2,160, yielding an impressive LTV:CAC ratio of 12:1. To combat financial risks, we will implement a rigorous financial monitoring process that will allow us to adjust our marketing expenditures and customer acquisition strategies in response to changes in conversion rates and churn. Our goal is to achieve a churn reduction strategy below 5% by enhancing customer engagement through personalized support and regular updates based on user feedback. Furthermore, we will explore diverse revenue streams, including add-ons for custom reports and API access, to bolster our financial resilience.

People Risks As a nascent enterprise, our human capital is pivotal to our success. The potential loss of key personnel or the inability to attract skilled talent poses a significant risk. Our projected hiring plan includes critical roles such as a full-stack developer and a customer success manager in Year 2, with an overall target of eight employees by the end of Year 3.

To mitigate these risks, we will prioritize creating a conducive work environment that fosters talent retention through competitive compensation packages, flexible working arrangements, and professional development opportunities. Moreover, implementing a structured onboarding process will ensure that new hires are seamlessly integrated into our company culture and operational workflows.

Contingency Planning Contingency planning is an integral part of our risk management strategy. In the event of unforeseen circumstances such as a significant market downturn or a failure to meet projected customer acquisition targets, we have outlined a series of actionable steps. These include temporarily scaling back on non-essential expenditures, pivoting our marketing strategy to focus on high-conversion channels, and exploring alternative financing options such as bridge funding or partnerships to sustain operations.

In summary, while FinFlow AI faces various market, technical, financial, and people risks, our proactive approach to risk management—underpinned by data-driven insights and strategic planning—positions us to navigate these challenges effectively.
