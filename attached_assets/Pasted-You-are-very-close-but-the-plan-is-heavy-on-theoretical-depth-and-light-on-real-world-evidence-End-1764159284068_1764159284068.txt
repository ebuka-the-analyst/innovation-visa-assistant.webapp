PART 1 Weaknesses in Your Current Business Plan (Critical Risk Areas)
These are the specific weaknesses in your plan that make rejection likely unless fixed:


. Lack of Traction (MOST IMPORTANT WEAKNESS)
Endorsers want real-world evidence that people want your product.
Your business plan currently includes:
•	No waitlist
•	No prototype users
•	No letters of interest
•	No surveys
•	No partnerships
Endorsers reject 80–90% of applicants who provide “zero traction.”
 
 2. No Proof of Founder Capability
Your founder background is very strong, but endorsers want evidence, not words.
Missing evidence:
•	GitHub link to your coding work
•	Live demo or screenshots
•	Past client references
•	Portfolio of completed systems
•	Video demo of MVP
Without these, endorsers doubt you can actually build the product.
 
 3. Missing MVP or Beta Demo
A detailed plan is not enough.
Endorsers want:
•	A working prototype
•	Or a partially functional tool
•	Or even a simple clickable mock-up
Your plan describes a system but does not show it.
 
 4. Market Numbers Need Verification
Some figures in your business plan (e.g., 15,000 yearly Innovator Founder applicants) are not correct.
Endorsers will reject a plan if market numbers are:
•	unrealistic
•	unverified
•	unsourced
This signals poor commercial understanding.
 
 5. Missing Commercial Validation
Examples of missing evidence:
•	No competitor comparison table
•	No user interviews
•	No case studies
•	No advisors onboard
Endorsers want proof that the business:
•	solves a real problem
•	has real demand
 
 6. Regulatory Risk: Immigration Compliance
Your tool helps users with visas.
Endorsers will worry about:
•	whether this crosses into regulated immigration advice
•	legal exposure
•	OISC compliance
Your plan mentions compliance but needs:
•	A legal opinion letter
•	Partnerships with OISC-regulated advisers
Without this, endorsers fear Home Office scrutiny.
 
 7. No Advisory Board
Endorsers strongly prefer:
•	1 immigration adviser
•	1 AI/tech advisor
•	1 business advisor
This is missing from your plan.
 
 8. No Evidence of Financial Resilience
You claim you can build the startup with minimal funding, but endorsers want:
•	bank statements
•	investment readiness
•	cost justification
You don’t need money, but you need a credible runway plan.
