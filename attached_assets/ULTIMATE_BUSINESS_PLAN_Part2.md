### 5.3 The Innovator Founder Visa Landscape

**Visa Overview:**
The Innovator Founder Visa was introduced in April 2023, replacing the previous Start-up Visa and Innovator Visa categories. It is designed for "experienced businesspeople seeking to establish a business in the UK based on an innovative business idea."

**Key Requirements:**

| Requirement | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| Endorsement | Required from approved endorsing body |
| Innovation | Business must be genuinely innovative |
| Viability | Realistic and achievable business plan |
| Scalability | Potential for growth and job creation |
| English Language | B2 level (CEFR) or equivalent |
| Maintenance Funds | 1,270 GBP for 28 consecutive days |
| Minimum Investment | None specified (removed April 2023) |

**Approved Endorsing Bodies (as of November 2025):**

| Organisation | Focus |
|--------------|-------|
| Envestors Limited | General business endorsement |
| UK Endorsing Services (UKES) | Consortium of six UK businesses |
| Innovator International | Supports 700+ entrepreneurs |
| Global Entrepreneurs Programme | Government-run, invitation-only for tech |

**Success Rates:**
- Overall success rate for well-prepared applications: approximately 88%
- Rejection rate at endorsement stage: approximately 60-70%
- Most common rejection reason: weak business plans (40% of rejections)

**Application Costs:**
