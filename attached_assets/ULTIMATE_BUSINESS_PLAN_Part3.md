## SECTION 8: BUSINESS MODEL AND REVENUE STRATEGY

### 8.1 Business Model Overview

UK Innovator Founder Visa Assistant operates as a Software as a Service (SaaS) platform, providing subscription-based access to visa preparation tools and resources. This model enables scalable delivery of professional-quality support at accessible prices.

**Core Value Proposition:**
Transforming a 5,000+ GBP service into a 29-129 GBP product through technology, while maintaining or exceeding the quality of traditional offerings.

### 8.2 Revenue Streams

**Primary Revenue Stream: Tiered Subscriptions (70% of Revenue)**

| Tier | Price | Target Segment | Value Proposition |
|------|-------|----------------|-------------------|
| Free | 0 GBP | Initial exploration | 13 essential tools, basic visa guidance |
| Basic | 29 GBP | Straightforward applications | 20 tools, core coverage |
| Premium | 49 GBP | Most applicants | 83 tools, comprehensive coverage |
| Enterprise | 89 GBP | Complex ventures | 109 tools, advanced features |
| Ultimate | 129 GBP | Maximum support | All features + VIP support + guarantees |

**Secondary Revenue Stream: Partnerships (20% of Revenue)**

Partnership revenue sources include:
- Referral fees from immigration lawyers for qualified leads
- Commission from endorsing body referrals
- White-label licensing fees from universities
- Affiliate revenue from related services (banking, accounting, etc.)

**Tertiary Revenue Stream: Premium Services (10% of Revenue)**

Additional paid services include:
- One-to-one consultation sessions with visa experts
- Expert document review services
- Priority support packages
- Custom enterprise solutions for organisations

### 8.3 Pricing Strategy

**Value-Based Pricing Rationale:**

The pricing is anchored against traditional service costs:
- Traditional immigration lawyer: 5,000-15,000 GBP
- Our comprehensive platform: 29-129 GBP
- User savings: 4,871-14,871 GBP (97-99% reduction)

This massive value differential drives conversion from free to paid tiers.

**Tier Differentiation Strategy:**

Each tier is designed to serve specific user needs:

Free Tier:
- Lead generation and product demonstration
- Low-risk trial for hesitant users
- Builds trust before payment
- Collects user data for targeting

Basic Tier (29 GBP):
- Entry point for budget-conscious users
- Sufficient for very straightforward applications
- Low price point reduces purchase friction
- Upgrades likely as users discover needs

Premium Tier (49 GBP):
- Core offering targeting majority of users
- Comprehensive coverage for typical applications
- Price point feels accessible for value received
- "Most Popular" positioning encourages selection

Enterprise Tier (89 GBP):
- Advanced features for complex cases
- Deep analysis and scenario planning
- Targets more sophisticated users
- Higher margin per transaction

Ultimate Tier (129 GBP):
- Maximum value and support
- VIP experience differentiator
- Premium margins for service costs
- Attracts users who want assurance
