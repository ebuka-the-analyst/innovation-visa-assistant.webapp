## 1. RISK MANAGEMENT FRAMEWORK

### Purpose

This document demonstrates comprehensive risk awareness and mitigation planning as required for the UK Innovator Founder Visa endorsement. It identifies key risks across regulatory, market, operational, and financial dimensions with detailed contingency strategies.

### Risk Assessment Methodology

Each risk is evaluated on:
- **Probability:** Low (under 25%), Medium (25-50%), High (over 50%)
- **Impact:** Low (minimal effect), Medium (significant but manageable), High (threatening)
- **Priority:** Critical (immediate attention), Medium (monitor closely), Low (periodic review)

## 2. REGULATORY & LEGAL RISKS

### Risk 2.1: Immigration Policy Changes

**Description:** The UK government could change Innovator Founder Visa requirements or eliminate the category entirely.

| Factor | Assessment |
|--------|------------|
| Probability | Medium |
| Impact | High |
| Priority | Critical |

**Mitigation Strategies:**
1. Maintain real-time monitoring of Home Office announcements
2. Build platform architecture to adapt quickly to changes
3. Develop content for adjacent visa categories as backup
4. Establish relationships with policy-informed sources
5. Plan pivot strategy to related services if needed

**Contingency Plan:**
- If visa category changes: Pivot to Skilled Worker, Global Talent, or Scale-Up visa tools within 30 days
- If category eliminated: Redirect platform to general UK business immigration support
- Maintain 6-month cash runway to weather transition period

---

### Risk 2.2: Legal Restrictions on Advice

**Description:** Providing immigration-related guidance could be construed as regulated legal advice.

| Factor | Assessment |
|--------|------------|
| Probability | Medium |
| Impact | High |
| Priority | Critical |

**Mitigation Strategies:**
1. Clear disclaimers stating platform provides information, not legal advice
2. Recommend users seek professional legal advice for specific situations
3. Partner with regulated solicitors for referral of complex cases
4. Consult with legal counsel on content and positioning
5. Maintain clear boundaries on what platform offers

**Contingency Plan:**
- OISC registration review if regulatory guidance changes
- Pre-drafted partnership agreements with 5 law firms for rapid referral system
- All content includes mandatory legal disclaimer

**Current Implementation:**
- OISC compliance disclaimers on all platform pages
- Export documents include legal disclaimer footer
- "Seek professional advice" prompts in critical tools
