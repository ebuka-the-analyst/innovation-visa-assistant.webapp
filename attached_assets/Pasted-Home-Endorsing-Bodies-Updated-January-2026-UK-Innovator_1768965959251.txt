What is an Endorsing Body?
An endorsing body is an organization approved by the UK Home Office to assess and endorse business ideas for the Innovator Founder Visa. They evaluate whether your business meets the innovation, viability, and scalability criteria required for the visa.

Receiving endorsement is mandatory for the visa application. Each endorsing body has its own application process, sector focus, and requirements. Choosing the right one can significantly impact your chances of success.
