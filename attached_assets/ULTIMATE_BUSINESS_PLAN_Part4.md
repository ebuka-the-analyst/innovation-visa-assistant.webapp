## SECTION 12: RISK ANALYSIS AND MITIGATION

### 12.1 Risk Management Framework

Effective risk management is essential for business sustainability. This section identifies key risks across categories and outlines mitigation strategies for each.

**Risk Assessment Methodology:**
Each risk is evaluated on:
- Probability: Low (under 25%), Medium (25-50%), High (over 50%)
- Impact: Low (minimal effect), Medium (significant but manageable), High (threatening to business)
- Mitigation Priority: Based on probability x impact calculation

### 12.2 Regulatory and Legal Risks

**Risk 12.2.1: Immigration Policy Changes**

Description: The UK government could change Innovator Founder Visa requirements or eliminate the category entirely.

| Assessment | Rating |
|------------|--------|
| Probability | Medium |
| Impact | High |
| Priority | Critical |

Mitigation Strategies:
- Maintain real-time monitoring of Home Office announcements
- Build platform architecture to adapt quickly to changes
- Develop content for adjacent visa categories as backup
- Establish relationships with policy-informed sources
- Plan pivot strategy to related services if needed

**Risk 12.2.2: Legal Restrictions on Advice**

Description: Providing immigration-related guidance could be construed as legal advice, which is regulated.

| Assessment | Rating |
|------------|--------|
| Probability | Medium |
| Impact | High |
| Priority | Critical |

Mitigation Strategies:
- Clear disclaimers stating platform provides information, not legal advice
- Recommend users seek professional legal advice for specific situations
- Partner with regulated solicitors for referral of complex cases
- Consult with legal counsel on content and positioning
- Maintain clear boundaries on what platform offers

**Risk 12.2.3: Data Protection Compliance**

Description: Failure to comply with GDPR and data protection requirements could result in penalties.

| Assessment | Rating |
|------------|--------|
| Probability | Low |
| Impact | High |
| Priority | Medium |

Mitigation Strategies:
- Full GDPR compliance in platform design and operations
- Regular data protection audits
- Clear privacy policy and user consent mechanisms
- Data minimisation principles in data collection
- Secure data handling and storage practices
